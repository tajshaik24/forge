# FORGE Support

Need help with FORGE, found a bug, or have an idea? **[Open an issue](https://github.com/tajshaik24/forge/issues/new)** and include your iPhone and Apple Watch models and iOS version. Screenshots help.

## Common questions

**The Watch app isn't on my Watch.**
Open the **Watch** app on your iPhone → **My Watch** → scroll to **Available Apps** → tap **Install** next to FORGE.

**The widgets say "Plan today".**
Open FORGE once that day. The coach plans the day and the widgets update.

**My workouts aren't in Apple Health or Fitness.**
Check **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → FORGE** and turn on Workouts, Heart Rate and Active Energy.

**The AI coach isn't available.**
The coach uses Apple Intelligence. Turn it on in **Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri**; until then a built-in coach plans your workouts.

**Crews won't load.**
Crews need iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on this iPhone.
